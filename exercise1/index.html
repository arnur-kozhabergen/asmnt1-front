Sid Vicious

1957 – 1979 · The face of punk rock

About Sid Vicious

Sid Vicious was born John Simon Ritchie in London in 1957. In 1977 he became the bass player of the British punk band Sex Pistols. With his spiky hair, leather jacket and padlock chain, he quickly became the face of punk rock. His life was short and chaotic: he died in New York in 1979, at only 21 years old. Today he is still a symbol of the rebellious spirit of punk.

What is punk?

Punk appeared in the mid-1970s in London and New York. It was fast, loud and simple music, and also a way to say no to the rules. Punks made their own clothes, magazines and bands. This "do it yourself" idea is the heart of punk culture.

Important events

  1. 1957 — born in London
  2. 1976 — played drums at the first show of Siouxsie and the Banshees
  3. 1977 — joined the Sex Pistols as the bass player
  4. 1978 — the Sex Pistols broke up after their US tour
  5. 1978 — released his own punk version of "My Way"
  6. 1979 — died in New York at the age of 21

Contribution to punk culture

  • Became the main visual symbol of punk style
  • Often credited with inventing the pogo dance
  • Showed the punk idea that anyone can start a band
  • Turned the classic song "My Way" into a punk anthem
